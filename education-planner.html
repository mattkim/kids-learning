Planning assumptions

All estimates are future dollars. Undergraduate costs include tuition, fees, books, transport and personal expenses. Room and board can be excluded with the checkbox. Graduate degrees use illustrative in-state public university rates.

How estimates work

College entry is estimated as birth year + 18. A four-year bachelor's is followed, if selected, by a two-year master's, three-year law degree, or four-year medical degree. A master's is not required for law or medicine.

Baseline annual costs in 2026 dollars: In-state public undergraduate $37,213 with housing ($18,355 without); out-of-state public $70,000 with housing ($50,000 without); private university $100,000 with housing ($74,500 without). Additional annual graduate study costs in 2026 dollars are modeled at $47,367 master's, $77,172 law, or $100,000 medical with housing, with estimated non-housing portions of $32,000, $61,000, and $84,000 respectively. Actual schools and programs vary widely.

Private preschool–12 tuition is modeled as two preschool years at $30,000 per year, nine K–8 years at $38,000 per year, and four high school years at $50,000 per year, inflated from 2026. Private-school figures are tuition only and do not include preschool boarding. The model assumes preschool starts at age 3, kindergarten at age 5, and college at age 18. Public K–12 tuition is modeled as $0, excluding taxes and incidental costs.

Illustrative planning model, not a tuition quote, financial advice, or a prediction. No scholarships, financial aid, taxes, or special residency exceptions are assumed. Some projected expenses may not qualify for tax-free 529 withdrawals.
